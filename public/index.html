Built as a Major Project at Bajaj Institute of Technology, Wardha

AuxQuiz is developed by the Department of Computer Engineering as a Major Project (BTCOS708), guided by Dr. Gajanan Tikhe.

It combines Retrieval-Augmented Generation, secure authentication and cloud storage into a single platform that turns study material into interactive, context-aware quizzes — reducing the manual effort of creating assessments.
